Code basis for every service

Every job on this list falls under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts. Whether a permit is needed depends on the job and the town, and the Enfield building department can confirm it. The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection licenses plumbers, so you can ask any plumber about their license before work starts.
