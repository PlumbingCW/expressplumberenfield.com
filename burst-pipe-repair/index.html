Burst pipe emergency: do this in the first ten minutes

Most of the damage from a burst pipe comes from how long the water runs before someone stops it. Know where the main shutoff is before an emergency, and test that it closes.

If water is flowing now, follow these steps in order.

  1. Close the main water shutoff. It is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the remaining water out of the lines.
  3. Switch off power to flooded rooms only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and tell dispatch.
  4. Move furniture, rugs and electronics off wet floors and photograph everything for your claim.
  5. Call (860) 397-8103 and say where the pipe is and whether it is hot or cold water.

Why pipes burst in Enfield winters

Water expands as it turns to ice, and pipe walls cannot absorb the pressure. The burst usually happens not at the ice itself but between the ice plug and a closed faucet. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages, and basements near uninsulated rim joists freeze first.

A burst can stay hidden while the pipe is frozen and then pour out when it thaws, which is why a house can seem fine at 6 a.m. and flood at noon. If you suspect a frozen line, keep the faucet slightly open and call before attempting to thaw it with open flame, which starts fires.

Every minute a burst pipe runs adds to the cleanup. Call first, then start moving belongings.

Frozen pipe repair: thawing without making it worse

A frozen pipe that has not burst can often be thawed safely with a hair dryer or heating pad, working from the faucet toward the frozen section. A pipe that has cracked needs a plumber, because thawing it just starts the leak. We check the pipe with a camera or pressure test before reopening it, and we cut out and replace any damaged section.

Repairs use copper, PEX or the material that matches the existing run. After the repair we look at why it froze, which may mean insulation, a heat source or rerouting a line. A pipe that bursts once in the same spot will do it again.

Code requirements for pipe repairs

A permanent pipe repair should follow the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts, including approved pipe materials and fittings. The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection licenses plumbers, so you can ask any plumber about their license before work starts.

Emergency shutoff and temporary stops come first, and code work follows once the water is controlled. If the cause is not obvious, water service line work or a failed water heater are common sources to rule out.
