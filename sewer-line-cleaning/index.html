Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Enfield, CT

Sewer line cleaning in Enfield, CT clears the main drain that carries every fixture's wastewater from your house to the town sewer or septic tank. When that line is blocked, the lowest drain in the house is the first to overflow, and our dispatch answers that emergency 24/7.

Until we arrive, stop all water use, keep the basement floor drain area clear and avoid walking through standing wastewater.

Sewer backup is an emergency: first steps

Wastewater in a basement is a health risk and a flooding problem at the same time. Floor drains, the lowest shower and the toilet nearest the main are the usual overflow points.

Do these things while dispatch gets a crew moving.

  1. Stop all water use, including the washing machine and dishwasher.
  2. Keep children, pets and bare feet out of the affected room.
  3. Open basement windows only if it is safe, and never use a wet-dry vacuum on sewage.
  4. Take photos before you move anything, and keep receipts for cleanup supplies.
  5. Call (860) 397-8103 and say whether this is the first backup or a repeat.

What blocks a main sewer line

Four causes account for most main-line calls: grease and wipes that were flushed, tree roots that entered at a joint, a belly where the pipe sagged and holds water, and a broken or collapsed section. The first two are cleaning problems. The last two are repair problems that cleaning only postpones.

In an older neighborhood like Thompsonville or around Enfield Village, the age and material of the lateral are not known from the street, so we confirm the pipe's condition when we clear it.

A main line that backs up once is a clog. One that backs up twice is a pipe problem, and the camera tells you which.

How we clean the line, step by step

We locate the cleanout, run a cable sized to a 4 inch main to break the blockage, and flush the line to test flow. If grease or scale is the root cause, a hydro jet follows. We then pull a camera through the line and show you the footage, so the decision about repair is yours and based on what is in the pipe.

When the camera finds roots at a joint, we cut them back and tell you how fast they are likely to return. Where it finds a crack or collapse, the conversation moves to sewer line repair with spot repair and lining as options.

Code and utility rules for sewer lines

The building sewer on your property falls under the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts, while the public main belongs to the sewer authority. If a blockage turns out to be in the public main, the utility handles it. The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection licenses plumbers, so you can ask any plumber about their license before work starts.
