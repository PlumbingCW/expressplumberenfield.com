Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website. We keep it short because we collect very little. Last updated November 14, 2026.

Information we receive when you call

When you call (860) 397-8103, we receive your phone number and the details you give us, such as your address and the problem. We use them to dispatch a crew, give you a price and follow up on the job.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell us at the start and we will help you in another way where we can.

Information on this website

This website has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to type in personal details. The site may log technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, through our hosting provider, for security and to keep the site running.

The site uses embedded maps and web fonts from Google. When your browser loads them, Google may receive technical data such as your IP address. Google's own policy governs what it does with that data.

How we use and share information

We use call information to dispatch and complete work, to answer questions and to improve our service. Work may be carried out by licensed plumbers who are dispatched through our line, so we share the details needed to do the job with them.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information when the law requires it or to protect safety.

Your choices and contact

You can ask what information we hold about your call, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@expressplumberenfield.com or call (860) 397-8103.

We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest version.

Our Enfield, CT service area

Dispatch runs from Enfield across the Connecticut River to Suffield and Windsor Locks, south to East Windsor and Ellington, and east to Somers.
