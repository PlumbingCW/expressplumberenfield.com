Before we arrive in Windsor Locks: burst pipe or active leak

In a multi-unit building, a leak in one apartment hits the ones below it, so the first step is also to warn the neighbors. Acting fast keeps the problem to one unit.

These steps help, whether you own or rent.

  1. Close the main water shutoff, or the unit shutoff if the building has one.
  2. Tell the neighbors below you and the landlord or building manager.
  3. Turn off power in wet areas only if you can reach the panel on dry footing.
  4. Photograph the leak, the damage and any belongings that got wet.
  5. Call (860) 397-8103 and tell dispatch which unit and who can give access.

Along the canal and the river: water you can see and water you cannot

Windsor Locks lies along the Connecticut River, and the canal and the Enfield Falls locks sit nearby. Properties close to the water see more groundwater after heavy rain, and basements are where it shows up first. A sump pump that fails in a storm leaves little time to react.

Winter and the older house

Rental buildings that run on a low thermostat to save on heat are where pipes freeze most often. A pipe in an exterior wall or an unheated hallway can split on the first hard night. If you manage a property, set a minimum temperature and tell tenants to report cold spots at once.
