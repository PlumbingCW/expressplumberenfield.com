Plumbing emergency in Enfield? Do this first

The first ten minutes decide how bad the damage gets. These steps apply to most emergencies, and dispatch walks you through them if you are unsure.

Gas comes first. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

  1. Close the main water shutoff, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. Stop using sinks, toilets and drains if wastewater is backing up.
  3. Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets, panels and appliances.
  4. Move belongings off wet floors and photograph the damage for your insurer.
  5. Call (860) 397-8103 and tell dispatch what you see and where.

An emergency plumber should answer the phone at 2 a.m. and tell you what to do while the crew is on the way.

Codes and licensing in Connecticut

Plumbing in Connecticut is governed by the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection licenses plumbers, so you can ask any plumber about their license before work starts. Whether a permit is needed depends on the job and the town, and the Enfield building department can confirm it.
