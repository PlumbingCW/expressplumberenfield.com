Where Scitico is

Scitico is a section of eastern Enfield along the Somers border, and part of it lies across the line in Somers. It was settled in the early 1700s, and the Hazard Powder Company built a powder mill at Powder Hollow here in 1835. The mill sites are now in Scantic River State Park, which has entrances on Hazard Avenue (Route 190) and Bailey Road.

A reference source also lists a subdivision called Green Manor in Scitico, about 4.5 miles from I-91. The distance matters for planning, because a crew coming from Elm Street needs real driving time to reach this edge of town.

Know your water and sewer before you call

Connecticut homes are not all on the same system. Some use a public water supply, some use a private well, and some are connected to a sewer line while others have a septic system. We have no record of which setup a given Scitico address uses, so we ask.

Your water bill shows whether a company supplies you. A property card from the town or your closing papers can show sewer or septic. Knowing this up front lets us bring the right tools and avoid a second trip.

Scitico callers can start with the street and the symptom, and tell us about the water and sewer setup if they know it.

If your basement is taking on water

This is general guidance for any house with a basement, not a statement about a particular street.

  • Stay out of standing water if outlets or appliances may be in it, and shut off power at the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  • Look for the source: a pipe, the sump pit, a window well or a wall.
  • Check that a sump pump, if you have one, has power and that its float moves freely.
  • Move stored items up and photograph the water level.
  • Call us with what you see, and we plan the repair around the cause.

Code and permits for well, sump and drain work

Work on a sump pump, a well connection or a drain line is covered by the same state code, the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). A permit depends on the job and the town, and the Enfield building department can confirm it.
