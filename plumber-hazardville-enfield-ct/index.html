Plumber in Hazardville, Enfield, CT

Hazardville started as a gunpowder company village on the Scantic River and has grown into one of the larger residential sections of Enfield. We serve homes in the historic core and in the newer neighborhoods around it.

A call here often starts with one question: which water company serves your house.

A company village that became a residential section

Hazardville grew around the Hazard Powder Company, which made gunpowder here from 1835 until 1913. The Hazardville Historic District, listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1980, covers about 1,075 acres along Hazard Avenue and nearby roads and includes the powder works sites on the Scantic River.

The district lists homes from the 1840s and 1850s on Hazard Street, along with a school, a church and the Hazardville Institute. Most of what we visit are ordinary family homes, and we treat a historic address like any other until we see what is inside the walls.

Housing counts and what they tell us

The 2020 census counted 2,751 housing units and 2,632 households in the Hazardville census-designated place, with 6,870 residents and a median age of 43.0. Reference sources describe newer suburban developments extending east toward the Somers line.

That mix means we see both older houses near the historic core and later construction farther out. We do not guess which is which from the street. The water heater, the supply lines and the drains tell us what each house has.

Hazardville homeowners can call with the street and the symptom, and we plan the first visit from there.

Your water supplier and why we ask

Connecticut's utility regulator lists Hazardville Water Company as one of the suppliers for Enfield, and public drinking water data lists its source as groundwater and its service area as including Hazardville, Southwood Acres and Enfield. Not every house here is on the same system.

Check your water bill to see who supplies you. If you want to know what flows through your tank and fixtures, your supplier can send its annual water quality report. We use that information when we advise on a water heater, but we do not assume it for your address.

Before the plumber arrives

These steps help in any Hazardville house, whatever its age.

  • Find the main water shutoff and make sure it turns, since a stiff valve is better found before an emergency.
  • Look at the tag on your water heater for its age, and tell us what you see.
  • Note whether the problem is in one fixture or in the whole house.
  • Photograph any stain, drip or pooled water and write down when it started.
  • Clear the path to the basement or utility area.

Code and licensing for Hazardville jobs

A water heater swap or a remodel in Hazardville falls under the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). The Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection licenses plumbers, and the state eLicense lookup at elicense.ct.gov lets you check any plumber before work starts.

Reaching Hazardville

Hazardville sits along Hazard Avenue, which is Route 190, in the eastern half of Enfield. Our base at 115 Elm St is in town, and Route 190 is the main road we use to reach this area.

Beyond the east edge of Enfield is Somers, which has its own page at Emergency Plumber Somers CT. Hazardville Water Company also serves parts of Somers, so some callers from both sides give us the same utility name.
